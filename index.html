The Operating Team Behind Campus Tech Programs

LGTM Club designs hackathons, buildathons, AI challenges, CTFs and developer workshops for engineering campuses, brings in the technology companies that sponsor them, and runs every hour of the event on the ground.

In code review, LGTM means "looks good to me". We named the company after that sign off, since every program we run has to earn it from the campus hosting it and the companies backing it.

From First Call to Final Report

Here is what LGTM owns at each stage of a program, and what stays with your campus.

01

Before the event

Challenge design, tracks and timelines, sponsor and technical partner sourcing, registrations and onboarding, the mentor and judge lineup, and promotion across campus developer communities.

02

On the day

An event control room covering participant support, mentor queues, sponsor coordination, the schedule, technical issues and every escalation, so your faculty can watch the demos in peace.

03

After the event

Judging closure, winner coordination, certificates and documentation, with a separate report for the institution and for each sponsor, plus follow up with the standout builders.

What stays with your campus

The venue and rooms, power and seating, security and housekeeping, food vendors and campus permissions. Everything else sits with our team.

Clear contracts on every side

Institutions and sponsors each sign their own agreement with LGTM, with deliverables written down before a single registration opens.

For Institutions

Host a flagship tech event without building the team behind it.

A serious technical program takes months of sponsor calls, mentor scheduling and operations planning. LGTM becomes your official execution partner and carries that load, while your team keeps the venue, permissions and campus setup.

Program design

Problem statements, formats, tracks and timelines shaped around your students and your department's goals.

Industry sponsors

Technology companies, technical partners, prize partners and outside experts, sourced and managed by us.

Mentors and judges

Engineers and founders from our network, scheduled, briefed and looked after throughout the event.

On-ground operations

Communications, schedules, participant support and a control room that solves problems before they reach your faculty.

For Technology Companies

Put your product in the hands of India's next engineers.

Students remember the tool they built their winning project with. LGTM designs a challenge track around your API, SDK or platform, runs a hands-on workshop before the sprint, and sends you a report on signups, projects built and the builders worth hiring.

Developer adoption

Workshops and tracks that get students signing up and shipping with your product in the same weekend.

Branded challenges

A track written around your API with your engineers on the judging panel, so every submission is a working use case.

Hiring access

Watch candidates solve real problems under a deadline, then meet the strongest teams directly.

Partnership tiers include Title, Technology, Developer, Workshop, Prize and Community partner. to see what each tier includes.

For Startups and Accelerators

Launching in India? Meet its developers first.

Founders and accelerator cohorts use LGTM as their India execution team. We run hackathons, build challenges and campus workshops around your product at engineering colleges, so you get early users, developer feedback and local visibility without building a distribution network from scratch.

Product-led programs

Formats like "Build with [Your Product]" or an India Developer Launch, designed around your growth target.

Users and feedback

Signups, activated users and structured feedback from developers who built something with your product.

Accelerator cohorts

Accelerators can offer India market access to a selected group of founders, with LGTM running every campus activation.
